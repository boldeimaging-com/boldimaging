# Every page and every image on this site is meant to be indexed.
#
# There is deliberately no Disallow here. The preview hostname is kept out of
# search with an X-Robots-Tag header at the Cloudflare edge instead, because a
# crawler that is not allowed to fetch a page can never see a noindex on it --
# blocking the crawl is what leaves bare URLs floating in results. Allow the
# crawl, refuse the index, and only on the preview host.
#
# The original WordPress robots.txt carried `Crawl-delay: 30`. It is dropped on
# purpose: Google ignores the directive outright, Bing honours it, and at one
# request per 30 seconds the 19 pages and 199 images here would take the better
# part of two hours to crawl -- a real handicap for a site that needs fresh
# discovery straight after a migration.

User-agent: *
Allow: /

# The index; it points at the page, portfolio, category and image sitemaps.
Sitemap: https://boldeimaging.com/sitemap.xml
